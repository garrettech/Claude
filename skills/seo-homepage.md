# Workflow 4 — Home Page

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Primary brand keyword (or apply keyword selection rule against top brand-level GSC queries)

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
Query GSC for top brand-level queries by impressions.
Query GA4 for homepage performance (sessions, engagement rate, conversions) to inform priority areas.

**Step 2 — Fetch & rewrite**
Fetch current homepage content. Rewrite:
- Hero headline and subheadline
- Value proposition block
- Category/collection highlight blocks with short SEO-bearing intros
- Brand story short-form
- Trust block (reviews, press, credentials — only if verified in brand voice JSON)
- Footer CTA

**Step 3 — Metadata**
Produce optimised SEO title tag (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤160 chars).

**Step 4 — Internal linking**
Suggest internal linking pattern across navigation and footer.

**Step 5 — Helpful Content review**
Apply Helpful Content checklist (see quality frameworks). Revise failing sections.

**Step 6 — Search Quality scoring**
Apply Search Quality scoring. Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 7 — Final delivery**
Return: final copy + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10.
