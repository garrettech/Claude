# Workflow 2 — Product Page

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Product page URL
2. Focus keyword (or apply keyword selection rule against URL)

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
Query GSC for queries landing on or related to this URL. Identify supporting keywords, CTR gaps, and position 5–20 opportunities to inform copy.

**Step 2 — Fetch & rewrite**
Fetch current page content. Rewrite in HTML for Shopify's product description field, preserving all verified product facts. Minimum 500 characters total.

Output:
- H1 / product name (plain text — Shopify renders from product title field)
- HTML product description body:
  - Opening `<p>` (primary keyword in first sentence)
  - `<strong>Top 3 Benefits</strong>` as `<ul>`
  - Product story / craftsmanship `<p>` block
  - Styling or gifting angle `<p>` block
  - CTA line as `<p>` with `<strong>` emphasis
- FAQs in plain text below HTML (3–5 Q&As)
- Alt text suggestions for primary product imagery (plain text list)

**Step 3 — Helpful Content review**
Apply Helpful Content checklist (see quality frameworks). Revise failing sections.

**Step 4 — Search Quality scoring**
Apply Search Quality scoring. Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 5 — Final delivery**
Return: final copy + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10. Ask whether more edits or another page are needed.
