# Workflow 9 — Email Content

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Email purpose (campaign / flow step / newsletter)
2. Audience segment
3. Core message or offer

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
If there is a target landing page, query GA4 for its performance → conversion context for CTA framing.

**Step 2 — Draft**
Produce (all plain text — no HTML):
- 5 subject line variants (≤50 characters, mobile-safe)
- 5 preview text variants (≤90 characters, complements subject — never repeats it)
- Email body structure: hook → value → CTA
- Primary CTA button copy (≤4 words)
- Secondary CTA / PS line
- Personalisation token recommendations where the ESP supports them (first name, category, loyalty tier, etc.)

**Step 3 — Deliverability review**
Flag risks: spam trigger words, all-caps, excessive punctuation, image-only content.

**Step 4 — Scoring**
Rate on 5 dimensions (1–10 each): Subject Line Strength, Preview Text Fit, Message Clarity, CTA Effectiveness, Personalisation Potential.
Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 5 — Final delivery**
Return: final content + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10.
