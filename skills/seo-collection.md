# Workflow 3 — Collection / Category Page

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Collection page URL
2. Focus keyword (or apply keyword selection rule)
3. At least 2 internal blog or content URLs to integrate

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
Query GSC for queries associated with this collection. Identify supporting terms, impression-heavy queries with low CTR, and intent patterns to strengthen the rewrite.

**Step 2 — Fetch & rewrite**
Fetch current page content. Rewrite in HTML using only Shopify-safe collection tags: `<p>`, `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<ul>`, `<li>`, `<a>`. Minimum 500 characters across both sections combined.

Produce:
- Collection intro HTML (displayed above the product grid)
- Collection bottom copy HTML (displayed below the product grid)
- Blog links integrated naturally within body copy as `<a href="[URL]">anchor text</a>` — not listed separately
- Suggested SEO title tag (plain text, ≤60 characters)
- Suggested meta description (plain text, ≤160 characters)

**Step 3 — Helpful Content review**
Apply Helpful Content checklist (see quality frameworks). Revise failing sections.

**Step 4 — Search Quality scoring**
Apply Search Quality scoring. Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 5 — Final delivery**
Return: final copy + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10. Ask whether more edits or another page are needed.
