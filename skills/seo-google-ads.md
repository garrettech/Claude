# Workflow 7 — Google Ads (RSA + Assets)

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Target landing page URL
2. Focus keyword or keyword theme
3. USPs and current offer

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
Query GSC for top queries landing on or related to this URL → inform headline themes and keyword variants.
Query GA4 for landing page conversion rate → context for CTA and offer framing.

**Step 2 — Draft RSA asset set**
Produce (all plain text — no HTML):
- 15 headlines (≤30 characters each): primary keyword in ≥3, brand name in ≥2, CTA in ≥2, USPs across remainder
- 4 descriptions (≤90 characters each): benefit + CTA
- Final URL + 2 display path fields (≤15 characters each)
- 8–10 callout assets (≤25 characters each)
- 4–6 sitelink assets (title ≤25 chars, 2 descriptions ≤35 chars each)
- Structured snippet header and values

**Step 3 — Policy review**
Flag any Google Ads policy risks: superlatives without proof, prohibited claims, excessive capitalisation, trademark issues.

**Step 4 — Scoring**
Rate on 5 dimensions (1–10 each): Relevance, Message Match, CTA Strength, Policy Compliance, Asset Variety.
Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 5 — Final delivery**
Return: final asset set + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10.
