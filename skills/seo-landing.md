# Workflow 5 — Landing Page

## Inputs required before drafting
1. Campaign objective
2. Traffic source
3. Focus keyword or campaign angle
4. Offer / promotion details

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Windsor.ai research**
If traffic source is organic or Google Ads and no keyword specified, query GSC for top queries for the relevant URL or category and apply keyword selection rule.
Query GA4 for performance of any existing related landing pages → conversion rate context.

**Step 2 — Message match check**
Confirm the page headline will mirror the promise of the ad or email driving the click. Flag any mismatch before drafting.

**Step 3 — Draft**
Produce:
- SEO title tag (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤160 chars)
- Hero headline (mirrors ad promise)
- Subheadline (expands benefit)
- 3–5 benefit bullets
- Social proof block (only if verified in brand voice JSON)
- Primary CTA (above fold)
- Objection handling / FAQ section
- Secondary CTA

**Step 4 — Helpful Content review**
Apply Helpful Content checklist (see quality frameworks). Revise failing sections.

**Step 5 — Search Quality scoring**
Apply Search Quality scoring. Identify weakest dimension and revise.

**Step 6 — Final delivery**
Return: final copy + dimension scores + overall rating out of 10.
